About

"Tools are the easy part. Adoption is the job."

Newtown, Sydney NSW

I'm an AI adoption consultant based in Sydney. I spend most of my time inside client teams — sitting with the people who actually do the work, mapping where time is being lost, and building AI solutions with human-in-the-loop design where trust matters. The goal is always the same: the team can run what I built after I leave.

Before consulting, I spent seven and a half years at a Sydney micro-SaaS startup running enterprise account management for brands including PPG Paints, Nespresso, and Unilever Food Solutions. I built and shipped an AI-driven landing page platform that cut production time by 90%. I have a business degree, not a computer science one — the technical depth came from necessity and curiosity, not a curriculum.

The through-line across everything I do: I care more about whether an engagement leaves a team more capable than when I started, than whether it produced a polished deliverable. Tools are the easy part. Adoption is the job.

Halcyon01

Personal digital twin — voice-native AI meta-agent

A multi-modal AI agent that takes natural language commands, finds the right tool via semantic search, and executes. Built to stress-test agent-accountability patterns at scale.

Ongoing personal project

The core architecture is a semantic-search tool router. A local ChromaDB vector database indexes a library of modular Python functions. When I give a command in natural language, the agent performs a semantic search, uses Gemini to extract the specific arguments from my phrasing, and executes. New tools can be added without retraining anything.

Around that core: a voice-native interface using asyncio for real-time interruptible conversations, vision capabilities via Tesseract OCR, a Tkinter GUI panel, and a Streamlit dashboard with an offline "rumination engine" that maintains a psyche profile (OCEAN model) to tailor assistance style.
